Account & Service Policy

Personal Account

A Healthcare Mauritius account is personal to the individual who creates and uses it. Users are responsible for providing accurate information and for keeping their account details up to date where necessary.

Your account is intended for your own use and should not be shared with another person. You are responsible for keeping your login credentials, passwords and other account access information confidential.

Account Security

You are responsible for taking reasonable steps to protect access to your account. Do not share your password, authentication information or account access with other people.

If you believe that someone has accessed your account without permission, you should change your password and contact Healthcare Mauritius as soon as possible.

Personal and Healthcare Information

Depending on the services used, your account may contain personal information and information relating to your healthcare activities. This may include appointment information, documents, insurance-related information, contact details and other information that you choose to provide through the platform.

Healthcare Mauritius does not assume responsibility for the accuracy, completeness or medical significance of information entered or uploaded by a user, healthcare provider, insurer or other third party.

Users should only provide information that is necessary for the service they are using and should take care when uploading documents or other sensitive information.

Appointments and Healthcare Services

Healthcare Mauritius may allow users to request, manage or otherwise interact with healthcare appointments and services.

An appointment request, booking or other interaction through the platform does not by itself guarantee that a healthcare service will be provided. Availability, scheduling, cancellations, changes and the provision of medical care remain subject to the relevant healthcare provider or organisation.

Healthcare Mauritius does not provide medical diagnosis, treatment or clinical decisions through this account service.

Insurance and Claims Information

Where available, users may be able to provide or manage information relating to health insurance, claims, supporting documents or reimbursement processes.

Healthcare Mauritius does not guarantee that an insurance claim will be accepted, reimbursed or processed within a particular period. Insurance decisions remain the responsibility of the relevant insurer and are subject to the applicable policy, documentation and requirements.

Users are responsible for ensuring that information and documents submitted for an insurance-related purpose are accurate and belong to them or that they have appropriate permission to provide them.

Documents and Uploaded Information

Users may have the ability to upload, store or share documents through their account, depending on the services available.

Users are responsible for the documents and information they choose to upload. This includes ensuring that they have the right or permission to upload and share information relating to another person.

Users should review documents and recipient information carefully before sharing them.

Your Choice to Share Information

Your account is designed to give you control over the information and documents that you choose to provide or share through available services.

Where a sharing feature is provided, you are responsible for selecting the intended recipient or organisation and reviewing the information before confirming the share.

Do not share medical records, identification documents, insurance information or other sensitive information with a person or organisation unless you intend to provide that information to them.

Healthcare Provider Information

Information associated with healthcare providers, appointments, services and availability may be supplied or updated by providers, organisations or other third parties.

Information may become outdated or may contain errors. Users should confirm important healthcare, appointment, availability, pricing or contact information directly with the relevant provider.

Not a Substitute for Medical Care

Your Healthcare Mauritius account is a service for managing information and accessing available platform features. It is not a replacement for professional medical care.

Information displayed in your account should not be interpreted as a diagnosis, medical recommendation or emergency medical instruction.

For urgent or emergency situations, users should seek appropriate emergency medical assistance rather than relying on the Healthcare Mauritius platform.

User Responsibility

You are responsible for the information you submit, the documents you upload, the appointments or services you request and the people or organisations with whom you choose to share information.

You must not use another person's account, impersonate another person, deliberately submit false information or use the platform in a way that could harm another person or interfere with the operation of the service.

Service Availability

Healthcare Mauritius may modify, suspend, restrict or discontinue features or services from time to time. Certain services may also depend on third-party healthcare providers, insurers, communication services or other organisations.

Healthcare Mauritius does not guarantee that every feature, appointment service, document service, insurance-related service or third-party connection will always be available or operate without interruption.

Account Suspension or Closure

Healthcare Mauritius may restrict or suspend an account where reasonably necessary to protect users, the platform, personal information or the operation of the service, or where an account is being used in violation of applicable terms or requirements.

Users may also request closure of their account subject to any information that must be retained where required for legitimate operational, legal or regulatory purposes.

Privacy

The handling of personal information through Healthcare Mauritius is described in the Privacy Policy .

The Privacy Policy should be read together with this Account & Service Policy when using an account or providing personal information through the platform.

Contact

If you have questions regarding your account, the services available through the platform or this policy, you can contact us .
