About

Healthcare Mauritius is a healthcare hub created to bring healthcare needs together in one place.

The platform brings together information about healthcare providers and services across Mauritius, helping people find and explore the healthcare resources they need.

Our aim is to make healthcare information easier to discover, navigate and understand, while creating a useful space for the healthcare community in Mauritius.
