About

Healthcare Mauritius brings healthcare services and providers together in one place, helping people find the healthcare resources they need across Mauritius.

From doctors, hospitals and clinics to pharmacies, laboratories, insurance, dental services and Sport & Fitness providers, Healthcare Mauritius is designed to make discovering healthcare services simpler and more accessible.

Our goal is to create a clear and useful place where people can navigate the healthcare landscape in Mauritius with greater ease.
